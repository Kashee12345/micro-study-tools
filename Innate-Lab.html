      Do a 20-cell differential

      Your jobClassify 20 white cells. Then read the percentages and decide what they suggest.

      Clues

      • Neutrophil: 3 to 5 lobes, pale granules
      • Lymphocyte: small; round nucleus fills the cell
      • Monocyte: biggest; kidney-shaped nucleus, grey-blue cytoplasm
      • Eosinophil: 2 lobes, large orange-red granules
      • Basophil: dark purple granules hide the nucleus
